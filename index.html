Share FitFlexr

It's just a web link — nothing to download, no account, no app store.

iPhone: open the link in Safari → tap the Share button (the square with an arrow) → scroll down → Add to Home Screen → Add.

Android: open the link in Chrome → tap the ⋮ menu (top right) → Install app (or Add to Home screen).

After that it opens like a normal app and works without internet.
